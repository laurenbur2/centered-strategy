Meet the founder

One partner, from first post to booked client.

Centered Strategy is led by Lauren Burandt. For five years she has grown founder and wellness brands on social, organically, including Bonetti Explosives from 500 to 265K followers and Kuya Wellness from 2K to 15K in a year.

She also designs and builds the websites, CRMs, and funnels herself. So the person planning your content is the same one building where it sends people, and nothing gets lost between agencies.

We started Centered Strategy on a simple belief: the best marketing doesn't manufacture hype. It tells the truth, beautifully.
