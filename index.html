How much does it cost?

Social media starts at $3,000 a month. Websites start at $2,000, and CRM and sales funnel setups start at $4,000. You'll get a fixed quote after our first call, so you know the full number before anything starts.

How long does a website take?

Most websites take three to six weeks from kickoff to launch. A CRM and funnel build usually adds four to six weeks, and we can run both side by side.

How much of my time will this take?

A kickoff call to start, then short reviews as we go. For social, most clients spend about an hour a week approving content. We handle the rest.

Do you only work with wellness brands?

No. Wellness and healing are where we go deepest, but we build websites, CRMs, and funnels for founders and teams in any field.

Is there a contract?

Social media starts with a three-month term so the strategy has time to work, then goes month to month. Build projects are paid by milestone, half to start each one and half on delivery.

Do I own everything?

Yes. Your website, accounts, content, and CRM are set up in your name. If we ever part ways, you keep all of it.
